🔎 Where do I find these numbers?

On a paper or PDF statement: the first page has a "Payment Information" box with your balance, minimum payment, and due date. Your APR is near the end under "Interest Charges". In your banking app: tap the card, then "Statements" or "Card details". Close enough is fine — you can edit everything later.

❓ How this works

Short answers, no jargon. Tap a question.

What do I actually do each month?
  1. Open the app. The colored banner at the top tells you the one thing to do next.
  2. Pay that card in your banking app, then tap ✓ Mark it paid here.
  3. Repeat until the banner celebrates. That's it — about 2 minutes.
  4. Every week or two, tap Update balance on each card so the numbers match reality.
What are the tabs?
  • 📋 Dashboard — what to pay next, and how every card is doing.
  • 🎯 Payoff Plan — type what you can afford monthly; it maps your whole way out of debt and tells every card what to pay.
  • 📈 Trends — your balance over time. The line going down is you winning.
  • 🔁 Subscriptions — import bank statements and it finds charges that repeat, and warns you before the next one hits.
How do I get reminders on my phone?

Tap 📅 Calendar at the top. It downloads a small file — open it, and your phone or computer calendar adds repeating reminders for every due date (alerts 2 days before and the morning of). Your calendar does the remembering, not you.

What do these words mean?
APR
The interest rate the card charges when you carry a balance. Usually 20–30%. It's on your statement.
Minimum payment
The smallest amount you must pay each month to avoid late fees. Paying only this keeps you in debt a very long time.
Utilization
How much of your credit limit you're using. Under 30% is good for your credit score.
Avalanche
Pay extra on the highest-interest card first. Saves the most money.
Snowball
Pay extra on the smallest balance first. Quick wins keep you motivated. Both work — pick the one you'll stick with.
0% intro APR
A temporary no-interest deal. When it ends, the real rate kicks in — the app warns you before that happens.
Is my data private? Can I lose it?

Everything lives in this browser on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere, ever — the app even works in airplane mode. Because of that, tap ⬇ Backup once in a while: it saves a file to Downloads that ⬆ Restore can load back (also how you move to a new computer). Clearing your browser's site data would erase the app's memory — the backup file is your safety net.

I made a mistake — how do I fix it?

Everything is editable. Tap Edit on a card to fix any number. Typed a wrong payment? Tap Update balance and set what the bank actually says — the app trusts you, not the other way around. Deleted a card by accident? Hit Undo on the message that pops up.
